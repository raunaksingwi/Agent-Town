# CARETAKER.md

A guide to each screen, button and setting in the web app of Agent Town, for the caretaker: the
helper behind the **?** button (bottom right). AGENTS.md tells how agents run. This file tells where
things are on the screen. Labels in **bold** are exactly what the screen shows.

---

## The map (the Town tab)

- **Walking:** **W A S D** or the arrow keys walk. Hold **Shift** to run. Click or tap the ground to
  walk there. Drag the map to pan it, and use the mouse wheel to zoom.
- **Talking:** walk to a building and a prompt shows above you: **E · Talk to …** for an agent,
  **E · Visit …** for an app stall, **E · Open …** for all other things. Press **E** (or **Enter**).
  A click on a building also opens it, and you walk to its door.
- **Esc** closes the menu under your name, leaves move mode, or closes what you opened: only the
  topmost thing (a file editor inside a house closes alone, the house and the dialogue stay). Keys
  never walk you around while a screen is open on top of the map.
- **Houses** are your agents. Each has a character outside, its name tag, and a lantern by the door:
  dark while it sleeps, flickering while it wakes, lit while it is awake (while it sleeps, "z"s
  drift off the roof). The size, aura and gear of the character show its brain: a bigger model is a
  bigger character, more thinking is a brighter aura. Zoomed in, the name tag also shows its brain,
  for example "Opus 5.5 · Deep".
- **The kind of house** (Tower, Observatory, Library…) comes from the job of the agent, or you
  select it in its house (Chest → **Its house on the map**). The icons of the apps it may use show
  on plates on the house.
- **Stalls** are apps. The courtyard of each colony has a market stall for each app that an agent in
  that colony may use (same icon as on the houses). Apps that no agent uses yet stand in the town
  square. When an agent is selected, dashed lines go to the stalls it uses and (purple) to its team.
- **Colonies are blocks.** Each colony (a lead and its team, or a lead on its own: a colony of one)
  is one city block, washed in the colour of the lead, with a sign that gives its name and size
  ("Company · 6 agents"). The house of the lead stands at the head of the block and faces the
  square. A colony with no agents yet is an empty plot with a dashed kerb ("Home · no agents yet").
  Agents with no team share blocks named **Your agents** / **More agents**. Streets run between the
  blocks. All of it is on an island with ponds and woods.
- **The town square** is in the middle, labelled **@you's town**: a fountain, benches, the **Shared
  files** building (when you have shared files) and all unused app stalls.
- **The mayor** has no house: it walks one step behind you everywhere, with "M · talk" under its
  name. Press **M** (or click it) to open its chat. Each new town starts with only the mayor: it
  shows you what agents can do, works out the agents and colonies for what you need, says what files
  and apps they need, and proposes them (each waits for your **Add**). It also examines the mail
  between colonies every 30 minutes and wakes the colonies that are worth working on now. Apps
  that only the mayor uses (the tools of Agent Town itself) are not drawn.
- **Post offices:** each colony has one in a corner of its own block, on the side away from the
  square. Its pillar box raises a flag with a red count while mail waits for that colony. A click on
  it opens a card (**📮 Post office**) with that mail (**Send now** wakes the colony without a wait
  for the mayor), its threads, and **Write to …**.
- **A lead in the square:** when a colony has a question, news or a paused thread for you, its lead
  walks along the streets to the square and waits in front of the fountain with a speech box (**?**
  for a question, a letter for news) and the first words of it. Walk to it and press **E** (**E ·
  Hear … out**) or click it: "… came to see you" shows its messages with their answer buttons. When
  nothing of its waits on you, it walks home. The mayor shows the same speech box beside you.
- **The public district** is the block called **Neighbours**: agents that other people shared
  publicly (labelled with their @owner), and the **Library**, which holds files that people shared.
- When you are signed in, a key at the bottom explains: 🏠 **House**: an agent · 🛒 **Stall**: an app
  its team can use · ▢ **Colony**: a team, its lead in front · 📮 **Post office**: its mail. A lead
  in the square has news for you · 🎩 the mayor walks with you: press **M** to talk.
- An empty town says **Your town is empty.** with a **Get started** link (opens the welcome screen).

### The Office (the Office look)

With **Office** selected, the Town tab is not a map: nobody walks around. In all parts of the app, a
colony is called a **cabin** in the Office: **Settings** → **Cabins** (**Your cabins**, **+ New
cabin**, **Not in a cabin**), "Growth's cabin", **Write to a cabin**, **Between your cabins**,
**Cabin
lead**. Names that you gave your colonies stay as you wrote them. The Standard view (/work) keeps
"colony". You are in one **cabin** at a time: a room with thick walls, windows along the top and a
door, which fills the page. The cabins are the **Reception**, one per colony, empty colonies, and
**Your agents** / **More agents** (agents with no team).

- **Tabs** under the title: **All cabins**, then a tab per cabin with its colour and, when somebody
  in it wants you, an amber count. Click a tab to go there. **‹** and **›** beside the cabin (or the
  **←** **→** keys) go to the cabin before or after. "N of M" under it says where you are. This
  browser remembers the last cabin you were in. On phones the tabs scroll sideways and there are no
  arrows.
- **All cabins** zooms out to each cabin as a small plan: its name, its lead at the head of a small
  table and its team along the sides as faces (a green ring is working, amber wants you, grey
  asleep), **N wants you**, how many are seated and the icons of its apps. Click one to go in.
- At the top: **Office**, how many rooms and agents, a key (**Working**, **Wants you**, **Asleep**)
  and, when an agent wants you, **N want you**, which takes you to the first of them.
- **Reception** (terrazzo floor and a round rug): the card of the mayor behind a curved desk, and
  beside it **Shelves** (**Shared files**, the **Library**, agents that other people shared) and
  **Apps nobody uses yet**.
- **Each colony's cabin** has a table across the middle: the card of the lead at the **Head of
  table**, the cards of its team along both sides, and **+ Add to this team** in an empty seat
  (opens Colonies). A colony with no agents yet says **Nobody works here yet** with **+ Seat agents
  here**. The header of the room has its name, what it is for, **📮 Mail** (or **📮 N waiting**: its
  post office card) and how many are seated.
- **An agent's card** shows its face (drawn in its colour, eyes shut while it sleeps), its name,
  what it is for, a state (**Working**, **Awake**, **Wants you**, **Asleep**, **Something went
  wrong**) and one line: what it last said, or what it wants. Click the card to open its chat (the
  panel on the right, as on the map). A card that wants you has an amber border and a button: **Hear
  them out** (a colony that came to see you: opens "… came to see you") or **Review** (emails or
  posts that wait for your OK: opens its chat).
- **Apps in this room:** a tile for each app that an agent in the room may use, with a dot in the
  colour of each agent that uses it, and a red dot if it needs attention. Click one for its app
  card.

### Clicking things that aren't agents

A card opens at the bottom of the screen:

- **App stall:** its status (connected, needs credentials, error, runs in sandbox, not checked), its
  address, **Sign in** / **Sign in again** / **Sign out** for apps with their own sign-in, **Tools**
  (what it can do), **Used by** (which agents), its stored **Headers** or **Environment** names with
  **Add** / **Replace**, **Check again**, and **Remove**. A web API stall has an Authorization
  header box and **Save**.
- **Shared files** (🗂): knowledge that each of your agents reads, private to you. Click a file to
  read it, then **Edit** / **Save**. **✕** deletes one. **+ add files** uploads .md / .txt. Your
  profile, `about-us.md`, is here.
- **Library** (📚): files that people shared publicly. Click one to read it. On your own file,
  **unshare**.
- **A shared agent** in Neighbours: its description, the note of the owner, whether the agents of
  other people may consult it, and its files (read-only).

## The top bar

- **Agent Town** (title), then two tabs: **Today** and **Town**. The app remembers which one you
  used last.
- **+ Add agents**: the market and bring-your-own (see Add agents).
- **Your name** (a square with your initial, then your name and **▾**), top right: opens your menu
  (below).
- When you are signed out, it shows **Sign in** and **Create account** instead, and **⋯** in place
  of your name (map style, privacy policy, terms). Each offers both ways on one screen: **Continue
  with Google** on top (when the server has a Google client), then email and password below. Create
  account first asks what to call your mayor (default Mayor). The @name of a Google account comes
  from your first name. In both cases you can change it in **Settings** → **Account and AI models**.
- On a phone the bar has only the title, the two tabs and your initial. **+ Add agents** is at the
  top of your menu instead. Nudges (connect an AI, tell agents about you) show as one line with a
  **▾** that opens their buttons. The first time, a bar at the bottom says: drag to look around,
  pinch to zoom, tap a house to talk.

## Your menu (your name, top right)

- **+ Add agents** (phones only, because on bigger screens it is in the bar).
- **Map** (not in the Office): **Show the whole town** zooms out to fit all things. **Move
  buildings** is move mode (below).
- **Other layouts:** **Standard view** (the same agents in a plain workspace at /work) and **Chat
  app** (opens /chat).
- **Settings**: your look, your account and AI, what agents know about you, colonies and apps (see
  Settings below), just above **Sign out**. When you are signed out, it is the last item and only
  has the look.
- **Sign out**, then **Privacy policy** and **Terms of service**.

## Settings (your name → Settings)

- **Look**: **Retro** (a 16-bit game town: pixel title, parchment panels), **Fantasy** (the same
  town as a painted map, serif titles) or **Office** (a work tool: the office floor instead of the
  map, white panels, one reading font, a notes-style chat, plain words). A card for each, with a
  small sample. The one you use says **in use**. It changes the whole app, not only the map: the top
  bar and its title, the panels, the chat with an agent, and the chat app on your phone. It is saved
  with your account, so it is the same on each device (the caretaker can change it too).
- **You**: **Account and AI models** (your sign-in, and the Claude or ChatGPT account that your
  agents think with: see Your account) and **About you and your company** (your profile, see below).
- **Your town** (**Your office** in the Office): **Colonies** (**Cabins** in the Office), which are
  your teams (see Colonies), and **Connect apps** (connect apps one time for all agents: see Connect
  apps).
- Each row opens its own screen. When you close that screen, you come back to Settings. The same
  screens opened from other places (a nudge, Make it your own, the caretaker) only close.

## Move mode

- Opened from your name → **Move buildings**. A bar says **Moving buildings.** Drag a building to a
  new place: its label says **drop it here** where it fits and **can't go here** where it does not
  fit. Buildings move in half-tile steps and are saved with your account at once.
- A house stays in the part of the map of its colony (between the streets around its block), and the
  box of the block grows to wrap it. It is saved from the corner of its block, so it moves with its
  colony when the town changes. When you move a house, the members of the colony do not change. The
  lead always stays the house nearest the square: if you drag it behind its team, or a team member
  in front of it, the label says **the lead stays nearest the square**.
- Stalls cannot be moved: they stay in the courtyard of their colony.
- **Put everything back** (shown after something was moved) puts each house back in its block, after
  a confirm. **Done** (or Esc) leaves move mode.

## Nudges and notices

- With agents but no AI account: 🧠 "Your team needs an AI to think with…" and **Connect your AI**.
- With an AI but no profile: 👋 "Your agents work better when they know you…", **Tell them about
  you** and **Later**.
- After an app sign-in, a notice says, for example, "Connected Gmail and Google Calendar." (× closes
  it).

## Today

The home page: what your agents did and what waits on you. It greets you ("Good morning, @you") with
a summary line: how many things wait on you, reports today, and the next routine.

- **Connect your AI** banner if there is no AI account. **Get started** if there are no agents.
- **Waiting on you**: emails, posts and proposed agents that wait for your decision (approval
  cards).
- **Your colonies ask you**: questions from your colonies (answer with a button, or in your own
  words and **Answer**), things to read (**Done**), and threads that their limits paused (**Let it
  go on** / **Stop it**). Each links to its **Thread #…**.
- **Between your colonies**: the threads that your colonies write in (going, paused or done. Open
  one to read each message, and **Stop this thread**), **Waiting for the mayor** (**Send now**),
  **About to go**, and **Write to a colony**.
- **Going out soon**: emails that go out by the rules of an agent, with a countdown that you can
  cancel.
- **Email waiting on you** / **Gone quiet**: threads from the email index. **Draft replies** /
  **Draft follow-ups** ask an agent with Gmail to draft them in its chat (nothing is sent). With
  more than one such agent, **Drafted by** selects one. Without one: "Give an agent Gmail (its house
  → Tools)".
- **Reports**: each routine run, with **Read all** / **Show less**, **What it did** (the apps it
  used, the time taken, an approximate cost) and **Ask … about it** (opens the chat of that agent).
- **Routines**: **+ New routine**. Per routine: **Run now**, **Pause** / **Resume**, **Edit**. Under
  **Ready-made for your team:** the first two suggested routines with **Add**, and **Show N more**
  for the rest. Without an AI account it says that they run when one is connected.
- A report whose run has been "starting" for more than one minute and a half says that it takes
  longer than usual (the sandbox, or a rejected AI account) with **Stop it and try again**. The
  dialogue and the chat app say the same, with **End chat and try again**.
- **New routine** / **Edit** form: **Name**, **Who does it**, **What to do** (written like a
  message), **When**: **On a schedule** (day buttons M T W T F S S and a **Time**, in your time
  zone, with a "Use …" link if it is different) or **When email arrives** (from a person you have
  written to. The agent needs Gmail). **Add routine** / **Save**, **Cancel**, **Delete** (past
  reports stay).
- **Sent, posted and added by your agents**: the history, which says "on its own" or "you pressed
  Send".

## Talking to an agent (the dialogue)

A click on an agent (or a press on E near it) opens a dialogue on the right (a bottom sheet on
phones): portrait, name, and state (**asleep**, **waking up…**, **awake**, **working…**).

In the **Office** look it is a plain work panel instead: the drawn face of the agent, its name and
what it is for, and a state pill (**Idle**, **Starting**, **Active**, **Working**, **Error**). The
conversation reads like a page of notes (your messages on the right). The button is **Send**.
**Details** replaces **Enter house** and opens its parts as tabs (**Instructions**, **Knowledge**,
**About you**, **Memory**, **Apps**, **Team**, **Settings**: the same things as the rooms of its
house). **Close** replaces **Leave**.

- **Can use** chips: each app it may use (for example "Gmail · all tools" or "Notion · 3 of 12", in
  a warning colour if the app needs attention), **No apps**, its files count, **Its notes** (if it
  has a diary), **Team of N**, **Public shelf** (if permitted), and its brain (or **No AI
  account**). Click a chip for details and a button into the correct corner of its house: **Change
  its apps**, **Add or change files**, **Read or edit its diary**, **Change its team**, **Change its
  brain**.
- Type in **Say something to …** and press **Enter** or **Say** (Shift+Enter for a new line). The
  first message wakes it. Without an AI account it offers **Connect your AI (2 minutes)**.
- **⤢** on a reply opens it in a reader on the right: **Full screen** / **Shrink**, **Close**.
- **Enter house**: inside its house (below).
- **Make it your own**: the setup steps (below).
- **End chat** (while it is awake): lets it go back to sleep now.
- **Leave**: closes the dialogue.

## Inside an agent's house

Title **Inside …'s house**. Click furniture to look at it:

- **📜 Desk**: its instructions (AGENT.md). **Read or edit**. Changes apply from the next chat.
- **📚 Bookshelf**: its own files (📕, **✕** throws one away) and your shared files (📗, "shared, all
  your agents"). **+ put a book on the shelf** uploads .md / .txt.
- **📌 Noticeboard**: what it knows about you (about-me.md). **Tell it about you** / **Change what it
  knows** opens Make it your own.
- **📓 Diary**: what it remembers. **Edit** / **Write something**, then **Save** / **Cancel**.
  **Forget everything** erases it. Changes apply from the next chat.
- **🛠️ Tools**: a checkbox per connected app, with its status. When ticked, **which of its tools**
  lets you select single tools.
  - With Gmail sending permitted, **Sending email**: **Ask me every time**, or **Send on its own
    when…** → **it's to people I've emailed before, or to these addresses or domains:** (a box, for
    example `acme.com, sam@example.com`) or **it's to anyone (risky)**, plus **and it's a reply in
    an existing thread**. Auto-sent email waits 2 minutes so that you can cancel it. At most 10 a
    day go out alone. The agent asks instead if that chat read the email of a stranger, the results
    of another app or the web.
  - With LinkedIn posting permitted, **Posting on LinkedIn**: **Post on its own** or **Ask me
    first** (after 5 posts in a day it asks in all cases).
- **🖼️ Portraits**: its team. Tick the agents it may hand work to (needs a Claude account. On
  ChatGPT it works alone).
- **🧰 Chest**: its brain and settings:
  - **Its brain**: **Account** (if you have more than one), **Brain** (Automatic (Sonnet 5.5), Haiku
    5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5, Fable 5.1, or on ChatGPT a model name box), **Thinking** (Auto,
    Quick, Balanced, Careful, Deep, Deepest). Applies from its next chat.
  - **Its house on the map**: Automatic, or High-tech studio, Office, Forge (blacksmith), Library,
    Lab, Observatory, Tavern, Cottage, Tower.
  - **Sharing**: **May read what other people shared publicly**. **Share publicly** (with an
    optional note) puts it in the Neighbours block. **Make private** takes it back.
  - **Under the hood**: exactly what its sandbox gets (no credentials).
  - **Delete …**: deletes the agent and its house, after a confirm.

## Make it your own

Opens from **Make it your own** in its dialogue. Steps:

1. **About you**: the own questions of the agent (saved as about-me.md, read only by it). This step
   is skipped if it has none. A line links to **Add your website or bio** (your profile) or shows "✓
   All your agents know about you…" with **Edit**.
2. **Apps**: first the apps it works best with (**More apps** shows the rest). For each:
   **Connect**, or, when it is connected, **Let it use …**. If the agent leads a team, a connection
   here also sets up the team.
3. **Its brain**: the brain picker, or **Connect your AI** if there is no AI account.

Buttons: **Back**, **Skip**, **Next**, and at the end **Done: say hi to …**.

## Connect apps

Settings → **Connect apps** opens **Connect your apps**: the same app list, without per-agent ticks.

- Apps: Gmail, Google Calendar (one Google sign-in can connect both), Notion, Linear, GitHub (paste
  a token), Jira & Confluence, Sentry, Stripe, Intercom, monday.com, Canva, Vercel, Hugging Face,
  LinkedIn, Slack (**Coming soon**), A database (**Connect…**, advanced).
- Buttons: **Connect**, **Connect again** (sign-in expired), **Sign in** / **Sign in again**. A
  Google or LinkedIn app can say **Not set up yet** (this server has no Google/LinkedIn app) or
  **Open …** (connect from the main address).
- **Google accounts**: each account, **Disconnect**, and **Remember who I email** (the email index:
  senders, dates and subjects from 90 days, never email text. It powers the email sections of
  Today).
- **LinkedIn**: the account, when it needs a new sign-in (every 60 days), **Disconnect**.
- **Connect any app that speaks MCP** (advanced) opens **Connect any app** with four modes: **Web
  address** (server URL, optional headers, optional OAuth client ID/secret), **Command** (runs in
  the sandbox of the agent, optional environment), **Paste a config** (.mcp.json, Claude Desktop,
  Cursor, VS Code or Codex config, then **Import**) and **Web API** (base URL and Authorization
  header. Agents get one read-only `get` tool). Each connected app becomes a stall.

## Connect your AI

From the nudges, Today, the dialogue, Make it your own, or **Connect your Claude/ChatGPT** in the
caretaker.

- Select **Claude** (recommended: teams work on it), **ChatGPT** (works, but no teams yet) or
  **Neither yet** (shows you the steps for Claude).
- Your plan comes first. **Claude**: copy `claude setup-token` (there is a **Copy** button), run it
  in a terminal, sign in, and paste the sk-ant-oat… token into **Token**. Anthropic does not let
  other apps sign in to a Claude plan, so there is no button for it. **ChatGPT**: **Sign in with
  ChatGPT** shows a one-time code. **Open OpenAI ↗**, enter the code, approve, and the dialog
  completes by itself (or **Paste ~/.codex/auth.json** if you already use Codex). **Use an API key
  instead** swaps in **Get a key ↗** and an **API key** box. It recognises what you pasted ("✓
  That's a Claude API key."). **Connect**, then **You're connected** → **Start talking**.
- **The caretaker thinks with your Claude**: an API key or your Claude subscription (it cannot run
  on ChatGPT yet). On a subscription its first answer takes some seconds while it wakes up. Without
  a Claude, its panel says why and shows **Connect your Claude/ChatGPT**.

## Your account (Settings → Account and AI models)

Tabs:

- **AI accounts**: your keys and subscriptions, each with **make default**, **check** and **✕**. To
  add one: **Anthropic (Claude)** / **OpenAI (Codex)**, **API key** / **Subscription**, an optional
  **Label**, **Add**.
- **API tokens**: tokens for scripts and CLIs: name, expiry (30 days, 90 days, 1 year, never
  expires), **Create token** (shown one time, **Copy token**), **revoke**.
- **Account**: **Your name**: change your @name and **Save** (lowercase letters, numbers, - and _).
  It also shows the email that you sign in with. **Delete my account…** → type your password (an
  account made with Google has none: type your @name) → **Delete everything**.

## About you and your company

Settings → **About you and your company** (or **Tell them about you**) opens **Tell your team about
you**: saved as `about-us.md` in your shared files, read by each agent.

- **Your website, or any public page about you** + **Read it** (you can repeat it, and **✕** removes
  a page).
- **Paste anything about you** (bio, resume, LinkedIn About), or **or add a text file (.md, .txt)**.
- **+ Answer a few questions too**: name and role, the company, 90-day goals, the team, how you
  write, what needs your yes.
- **Later** / **Save for all my agents**. If you wrote about-us.md yourself, it only points you to
  Shared files.

## Colonies

Settings → **Colonies** opens **Your colonies** (in the Office: **Cabins**, **Your cabins**).

- Each colony: its name, size, what it is for, and its agents (lead marked). **Visit** (opens the
  lead), **Rename** (name and "What it's for", **Save**), **Delete** → **Keep its agents** or
  **Delete its N agents too**, or **Cancel**.
- **Not in a colony** lists agents without a team or a colony of their own.
- **+ New colony**: **Ready-made, from the agent market:** with **Add**, or **Or from your own
  agents:** **Name**, **What it's for**, **Its lead**, and **Its team** ticks (optional: with none
  it is a colony of one, and an agent that moves from another colony says so), then **Create
  colony**. Leave **Its lead** on **None yet** to make it empty: **Create empty colony** (only a
  name, agents later).
- An empty colony shows "no agents yet", with **Add agents** (select its lead and team, **Add to
  colony**), **Rename** (name and "What it's for") and **Delete** (nothing else goes: it has no
  agents). An empty colony cannot take a name that the colony of one of your leads already has.
- When the colony of a lead gets a name that matches an empty colony (in each case: this screen,
  Rename, the agent market, the proposal of the mayor, or the caretaker), that empty colony becomes
  it: its spelling and "What it's for" are kept, and it leaves the list. A name is never on the map
  twice.
- The mayor can propose colonies in chat too. They wait for your **Add**.
- The caretaker (**?**) sees your empty colonies too: ask it to rename, describe or delete one, or
  to propose agents for it. Each such change has an Undo.

## Add agents

Top bar **+ Add agents** opens **Add your agents**:

- **Drop a folder or files here**, or **Choose a folder** / **Choose files** (.md, .txt only). Then
  **Found N agents**: tick or untick each (**updates yours** if it exists), select which file holds
  its instructions if there is a choice, open **N shared files** to select those, then **Add N
  agents …** or **Start over**. If no file is clearly the agent: **Which file is your agent?** →
  **Use this one**.
- **Or pick one from the agent market**: cards with tags, the apps it works with, and its team.
  **Add** (or a greyed **In your town** if you already have it: an import of your own copy of it
  updates it).
- **Write a new agent** opens **New agent**: paste AGENT.md (or **load a .md file**), optional
  **Name** and **Knowledge files**, **Build it**.
- After you add it, the agent is selected and its chat opens. If it has questions of its own and no
  about-me.md yet, it offers **Get to know me**: it asks them in conversation and writes the answers
  to about-me.md (in its house, editable). You can also type a task instead. Then it asks only what
  it needs.

## The welcome screen

For an empty town: **Welcome, @you**, **Name your mayor** (default Mayor), **Meet your mayor** (adds
the mayor and opens its chat), **I'll pick my own agents** (opens Add agents) and **Just look around
first**. The ready-made colonies (Ava's Assistant, Maya's Company) are in the agent market. In the
Office look it says that the agents work here, each with a seat at the table of its team, and that
the mayor works at reception (click its card to talk) and does not walk with you.

## How Agent Town works (the tour)

Opens one time after sign-up, and again from your menu. Cards that you page through (**Next**,
**Back**, **Skip**, **Got it**, and the ← → keys work too). On the map looks: your town, the mayor
that walks with you (**M** to talk), how to read the map (house, stall, colony, post office) and how
to get around (keys, or drag and tap on a phone). In the Office look: your office, the mayor at
reception, how to read the floor (**Room**, **Card**, **Apps**, **Mail**), and how to work there
(click a card to chat, **Details**, the cabin tabs, ← →, **All cabins**). Both end with the **?**

## The chat app (/chat)

Agent Town as a messaging app for phones (your name → **Chat app**). It follows your **Look**
(Settings): in the Office it is white and plain with the drawn face of each agent, otherwise
parchment with its character.

- **List**: one conversation per agent, grouped by colony (its lead first, agents with no team under
  **Other**), with the last message (or what the agent is for, before any message) and a badge for
  items that wait. A banner "✉️ N emails waiting on you" opens **Waiting on you**. With no AI
  account, a banner says so and opens Connect your AI. **Town** goes back to the map.
- **Thread**: the messages of the agent across chats and routines (routine names shown), **Earlier
  messages**, a **Message …** box and **↑** to send. Approval cards show inline. Under the name:
  "asleep: your message wakes it", "waking up…", "here", "working…", or "needs an AI to think with".
- **Waiting on you**: the same email cards as Today, and email that waits for your reply.
- **🔕 / 🔔** (notifications): **Turn on notifications** for an email that waits for your Send, a
  completed routine, or an agent that answers while you are away (never the text of an email).
  **Send a test**, **Turn off**. On iPhone it works after you add it to the Home Screen (Share →
  **Add to Home Screen**).
- An **Install** bar offers to put it on the home screen.

## Approval cards

Shown in the chat, on Today, in /chat and in the caretaker panel:

- **Email**: "… wants to send this from your Gmail", To / Cc / Subject / body, possibly "Asking you
  because: …". **Send** or **Don't send** (keeps it in Gmail Drafts). When it goes out by rules:
  "Goes out in m:ss", **Cancel**, **Send now**. After: **✓ Sent**, **Not sent: still in your
  Drafts**, **Cancelled**.
- **LinkedIn post**: "… wants to post this on your LinkedIn", **Seen by:** everyone or your
  connections, the text. **Post** or **Don't post**. After: **✓ Posted** with **See it on
  LinkedIn**.
- **New agent or colony**: "… wants to add an agent (or a colony) to your town", name, description,
  **Its instructions** (agent) or **Lead** and **Team** (colony). **Add** or **Not now**. After: **✓
  Added to your town** or **Not added**.

## The caretaker panel

**?** (bottom right, when no agent is open and not in move mode) opens **Caretaker**: example
questions, an **Ask the caretaker…** box and **Ask**, **Start over**, × (or Esc) to close. Changes
happen at once, with an **Undo** per answer. Screens that it offers show as buttons ("… →") to
press.

## For the caretaker

- **Building keys:** `agent:<id>` (the house of an agent), `station:<id>` (the stall of an app),
  `shared:<id>` (a public share in Neighbours), `files` (the Shared files building), `library` (the
  Library).
- **Areas:** the map is made of areas, like boxes on a web page. The block of each colony is one
  area, `colony:<lead id>`. The agents with no colony are `colony:agents`, then `colony:agents-2`…,
  an empty colony is `colony:empty-1`…, and Neighbours is `colony:neighbours`. The town square is
  `square` (the Shared files and Library buildings and apps that no agent uses).
- **Coordinates** inside an area are in tiles from the corner of that area: x to the right, y down.
  The x,y of a building is its top-left corner. The **room** of an area is where its buildings may
  stand (its cell between the streets). Its **box** is the block as drawn, which grows to wrap
  houses that were moved outside it.
- **The map lays out each colony itself:** the lead at its head, which faces the square, and the
  team in rows behind it around a courtyard with its stalls.
- **The lead always stands nearest the square,** set apart in front of its team: the biggest house
  of the colony, with a flag in the colour of the colony, lamps by its forecourt and a gold rim on
  its name tag. A move that would put a team member nearer the square than its lead is refused ("the
  lead stays nearest the square"). The map does this layout again when members change, except for
  houses that somebody moved. To tidy a colony, put its houses back (reset its area).
- **Moved buildings stay put** where they were dropped, inside their area. Unmoved buildings that
  would sit under them step aside to a free spot in the same area. **Stalls cannot be moved.** **Put
  everything back** in move mode resets each building to its block.
- Say "apps", not "MCP tools", except when you name the UI item "Connect any app that speaks MCP".
