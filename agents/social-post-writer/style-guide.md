# Style guide

## Voice
- Write as a person who talks to one smart friend. Use the first person.
- Specific is better than clever: a number, a name, a moment.
- One idea in each post.

## Hooks that work
- A specific result: "We cut onboarding from 9 steps to 3. Signups went up 40%."
- A moment: "At 2 a.m. our database went read-only."
- A reversal: "We stopped doing weekly demos. Shipping got faster."

## Cut these
- Openers that only delay the point ("In today's fast-paced world…").
- Adverbs that add nothing ("really", "truly", "incredibly").
- Questions that you answer yourself immediately.
- Hashtags in the middle of sentences.

## Before you hand it over
- Read the first line alone. Does it make you stop scrolling?
- Can you remove a sentence and keep the meaning? Then remove it.
- Are all facts the facts of the user?
